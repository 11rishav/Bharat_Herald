# 📰 Bharat Herald Business Analysis using SQL

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the operational and financial performance of **Bharat Herald** using SQL. The objective is to understand the company's declining print business between **2019 and 2024**, identify key operational challenges, and uncover opportunities for digital transformation.

The analysis simulates a real-world business scenario where a legacy newspaper organization is struggling with declining circulation, falling advertising revenue, operational inefficiencies, and increasing competition from digital-first news platforms. SQL was used to generate actionable insights that support business recovery and strategic decision-making.

---

# 📖 Problem Statement

Bharat Herald is a legacy newspaper organization with operations across five Indian states. With over **70 years** of multilingual journalism, the company once printed more than **1.2 million newspapers daily**.

Between **2019 and 2024**, the organization experienced a significant business decline due to:

- Shift from print newspapers to digital news consumption
- Failure of the 2021 e-paper initiative
- Growing competition from digital-first news platforms
- Declining advertising revenue
- Falling print circulation
- Vendor payment delays
- Closure of multiple city bureaus
- Workforce reductions across editorial and sales teams

To understand the causes of this decline, Executive Director **Tony Sharma** assigned **Peter Pandey**, a Data Analyst, to analyze operational and financial data from **2019–2024**.

The objective is to quantify business performance, identify recovery opportunities, and recommend a phased roadmap for digital transformation.
